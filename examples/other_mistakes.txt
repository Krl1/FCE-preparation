# Przykładowe dane demonstracyjne — luźne notatki z powtarzanymi błędami.
# Format jest DOWOLNY, tak jak w writing_mistakes.txt.

1. Gerund vs. infinitive
It's not worth to go there -> It's not worth going there. (zawsze: worth + -ing)
I recommend to go -> I recommend going.
I am not a fan of get a tan -> I'm not a fan of getting a tan. (po przyimku zawsze -ing)

2. Przyimki
Depends from -> Depends ON.
From the other hand -> ON the other hand.
On the gym -> AT / IN the gym.
In Friday -> ON Friday.
I was on the wedding -> I was AT the wedding.

3. Liczba mnoga i policzalność
The teeths are expensive -> The teeth are expensive.
Every children -> Every child / All children.
It's a lot of damages -> It has done a lot of damage. (damage zwykle niepoliczalne)

4. Przedimki
The Krakow -> Krakow. (miasta bez "the")
The nature affects me -> Nature affects me. (ogólnie — bez "the")
Popular places in the Italy -> in Italy.

5. Dobór słów
Consumptionism -> Consumerism.
Preferation -> Preference.
Boarding games -> Board games.
Traditional healthy kitchen -> Healthy cuisine. (kitchen to pomieszczenie)
The photos tell that -> The photos show / depict.
