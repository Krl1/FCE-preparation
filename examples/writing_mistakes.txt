"Essay (Technology at school) — Estimated level: B1+/B2 (5.5–6/10)

What works
Clear opinion and logical paragraphing.
Relevant examples.

Key problems
Grammar
"we started use computers" -> we started using computers
"a new methods were introduced" -> new methods have been introduced
"The technology helps" -> Technology helps (no article for general nouns)

Word forms and spelling
"comunication" -> communication
"more easier" -> easier

Tip: watch articles and present perfect for changes over time."

"Report (School library) — Estimated level: solid B2 (6–7/10)

What works
Good structure with headings, appropriately formal tone.

Key problems
"is closes at 6 p.m." -> closes at 6 p.m.
"too muche to choice" -> too much choice
"collage" -> college

Tip: push for more formal phrasing — "It would be beneficial to..." instead of
"It would be a good idea to..."."
